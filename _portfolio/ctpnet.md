**Venue:** IEEE International Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP) 2026

- **Abstract:** We propose CTPNet, which decomposes multivariate long-term forecasting into channel-, trend- and periodic-wise representation learning. Each view is processed by a dedicated lightweight Transformer-based module, and the fused representations yield competitive accuracy on standard benchmarks.
- **Core Idea:** Separate the channel, trend and periodic structures of multivariate series, and learn each with a minimal dedicated representation module.
- **Domain:** Time Series Forecasting, Deep Learning
