**Venue:** IEEE Transactions on Cognitive Communications and Networking (TCCN) 2026

- **Abstract:** We propose DST-ND, a diffusion-based spatio-temporal channel prediction framework that decouples the non-stationarity of wireless channels. A spatio-temporal diffusion model captures both temporal evolution and spatial correlation, improving prediction robustness in non-stationary scenarios.
- **Core Idea:** Decouple channel non-stationarity before diffusion, so the generative model only needs to learn the stationary residual dynamics.
- **Domain:** Wireless Channel Prediction, Diffusion Models, 6G Communications
