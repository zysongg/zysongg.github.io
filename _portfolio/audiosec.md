**Venue:** ACM ICCIP 2025; Knowledge-Based Systems 2026; Speech Communication 2026

- **Abstract:** A series of works applying Simplifying Graph Convolution (SGC) to audio security: (i) an audio deepfake detection framework combining SSL features with spectral-temporal attention and SGC; and (ii) a hybrid-domain audio watermarking scheme using the same graph-convolution backbone for robustness against common signal perturbations.
- **Core Idea:** Replace heavy GNN layers with simplified graph convolution, keeping graph structure modeling at negligible parameter cost.
- **Domain:** Audio Security, Graph Neural Networks, Speech Processing
